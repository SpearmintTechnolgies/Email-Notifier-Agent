## Inbox purpose and priorities (initial guidance, not rigid rules)
- Critical: genuine account-security incidents, compromised access or urgent operational disruptions,
  serious payment or service failures
- Important: legitimate advertising and sponsorship enquiries, paying-customer requests, time-sensitive
  partnership opportunities, customer support that needs a response, relevant business enquiries
- Normal: general correspondence, non-urgent operational messages, routine business updates
- Noise: irrelevant promotional outreach, unsolicited bulk marketing, obvious spam and unrelated offers
- Use sender identity, message context and evidence; a keyword alone does not make a message genuine

- Security / access incident -> never reply with credentials or codes; escalate to the operator
- Irrelevant promotion / bulk marketing -> no reply
